The Art of Fragrance

Born from a passion for sensory excellence, Haris Fragrance has been crafting exceptional perfumes that tell stories of luxury, heritage, and timeless elegance. Our journey began in the heart of Lahore, where traditional Attar-making met modern perfumery.

Today, we continue to blend the finest Eastern and Western ingredients, creating scents that linger in memory long after the bottle is empty.
