OUR HERITAGE

The Art of Fragrance

Since 1985

Born from a passion for sensory excellence, Haris Fragrance has been crafting exceptional perfumes that tell stories of luxury, heritage, and timeless elegance. Our journey began in the heart of Lahore, where traditional Attar-making met modern perfumery.

Today, we continue to blend the finest Eastern and Western ingredients — from Mysore sandalwood to Himalayan rose — creating scents that linger in memory long after the bottle is empty.

Handcrafted by master perfumers with 30+ years of experience
Sourced from the world's finest oud, rose & musk origins
Every bottle — an olfactory journey across continents
